Research notes

How the test works

Signal and sizing

Keep each stock at or below its starting basket weight. Hold the allocation in cash if its 63-session return is nonpositive. If 20-session annualized volatility exceeds 20%, scale the target by 20% divided by observed volatility.

Execution and costs

Set orders after the first observed close each month and model fills at the next observed open. Charge 5 bps in fees plus 10 bps in slippage per side. Cash earns zero. Compare each market in its own currency.

Historical inputs

Yahoo Finance adjusted close and OHLC supply a total-return price proxy. Two NSE sessions are absent. Vendor adjustments and a retrospectively chosen basket limit what this replay can establish. Yahoo Finance methodology ↗

Validation boundary

In each fold, six parameter pairs compete using earlier dates only. The simulation resamples development returns in contiguous blocks. No corrected statistical edge is established. The single-use holdout was never queried for this report. Moving-block bootstrap ↗
